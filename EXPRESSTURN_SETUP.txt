MESSENGER PRO V45.2 - EXPRESS TURN SETUP

1. Open turn-config.js in Notepad.
2. Keep the two TURN URLs unless your ExpressTURN dashboard shows a different server hostname.
3. Replace only:
   PASTE_YOUR_EXPRESSTURN_USERNAME_HERE
   PASTE_YOUR_EXPRESSTURN_PASSWORD_HERE
   with the Username and Password shown in your ExpressTURN dashboard.
4. Save the file.
5. Open/reload Messenger Pro and test a call.

IMPORTANT SECURITY:
- Never post your TURN username/password publicly.
- Do not put the TURN Secret Key in this file.
- ExpressTURN documents free.expressturn.com:3478 and supports UDP/TCP TURN authentication.

TESTING:
- Test from two different networks, e.g. PC Wi-Fi + phone mobile data.
- If the call still says Connecting..., open Chrome chrome://webrtc-internals
  during the call. A successful TURN-relayed connection should show a selected
  candidate pair using relay.
