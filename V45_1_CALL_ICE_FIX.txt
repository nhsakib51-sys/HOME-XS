V45.1 CALL / ICE FIX

Fixed the WebRTC signaling race where the caller created the Firebase call record only after setLocalDescription().
Chrome can emit ICE candidates immediately after setLocalDescription(), so early candidate writes could be rejected because the parent call node did not exist yet.

The call record is now created first (without status), then the local offer is created, and the record is updated to status=calling with the offer.
This keeps Firebase Rules satisfied and makes early ICE candidate writes reliable.

Also added ICE/connection state diagnostics and explicit Connected status handling.

IMPORTANT: Cross-network calls still require a valid TURN service when direct STUN/host/reflexive ICE candidates cannot connect. Configure turn-config.js with your TURN provider credentials.
